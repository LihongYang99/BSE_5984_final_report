# Literature synthesis and contribution audit

Reviewed November 24, 2026. This is a targeted narrative review for the course report, not a systematic review or an exhaustive novelty search. It adds context without changing model inputs, results, or approved scenarios.

## Scope and selection

The review asks what previous shrimp LCAs establish about feed, electricity, FCR, and environmental trade-offs, and what this particular reanalysis contributes. Four directly relevant primary reports were selected from the supplied literature and the existing evidence review. Eligibility required a shrimp LCA that informs the present scenario design, system comparison, or interpretation. Digital-twin and unrelated commodity studies were outside this focused scope.

Al Eissa et al. and Arbour et al. share the Indiana farm background. They are linked reports, not two independent observations of farm performance. No pooled effect estimate or formal risk-of-bias score is calculated.

## Contribution wording

The supported claim is a reproducible, case-specific decomposition of published US inventories under a common feed, delivery distance, grid factor, and comparison scope. The fixed-FCR case isolates the modeled electricity-demand effect. The literature-FCR case identifies the feed-efficiency offset, and separate grid scenarios distinguish farm from feed-mill changes. The completeness audit makes the ranking conditional on unresolved burdens.

The report does not claim the first shrimp LCA, discovery of feed/electricity hotspots, first grid/FCR sensitivity analysis, a new LCA method, or new empirical observations. The internal MJ-to-kWh correction is quality control and does not imply an error in the source literature. Restored transport/fertilizer inputs were already reported by the source and are not newly discovered processes.
