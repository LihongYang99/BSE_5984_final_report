Figure. LCA framework and its application to the U.S. shrimp aquaculture screening study.

The four phases are goal and scope definition, life cycle inventory analysis, life cycle impact assessment, and interpretation. Bidirectional arrows represent iteration and feedback. The project implements a partial GWP screening inventory with explicit proxies. Alabama uses the common Feed C supply chain as a counterfactual. The fixed-FCR comparison separates the electricity-demand contrast from feed efficiency. This diagram does not assert ISO conformity or complete life-cycle inventory coverage.

Framework source. International Organization for Standardization. (2006). Environmental management—Life cycle assessment—Principles and framework (ISO 14040, 2006). https://www.iso.org/standard/37456.html
