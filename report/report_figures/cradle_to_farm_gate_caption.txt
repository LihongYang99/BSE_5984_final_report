Intended cradle-to-farm-gate boundary and inventory coverage.

Blue boxes show inputs, green boxes show production processes, and purple boxes show outputs. Solid outlines indicate included coverage, dash-dot outlines indicate an optional scenario, dotted outlines indicate literature-based screening estimates, and dashed outlines indicate burdens not yet quantified. These burdens are not assumed to be zero.

The reference product is 1 kg live-weight shrimp at the farm gate. Upstream factors include supply-chain GHG emissions. Hatchery production, infrastructure, land-use change, direct N2O and pond CH4 have screening ranges in kg CO2-eq per kg shrimp from Appendix B, Table B3 (Step 43). These ranges are not added to the covered subtotals. Indiana sludge fate is not yet quantified. Only the optional Indiana water-treatment proxy is characterized for farm water, and Alabama pond-fill pumping is screened below 0.04. The diagram describes the expanded inventory. The separate controlled comparison includes feed supply and farm electricity only. Arrows show connections and are not scaled to quantities.

Farm inputs follow Al Eissa et al. (2022) and Appendix A. The coverage treatment is documented in Appendix B.
